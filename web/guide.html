You don't need an agent

Visa consultants in places like India routinely charge ₹2–3 lakh (~$2,000–3,500) to fill out forms that are, honestly, just forms. Here's the actual process, and what it really costs when you do it yourself.

Informational only, not legal advice. If your situation is complicated (prior denials, unusual history, dependents, etc.), a real immigration attorney is worth paying for — that's different from paying an agent to type your DS-160.

The actual steps

  • 1
    Get your I-20 or DS-2019

    Issued directly by your school or program sponsor once you're admitted. No agent involved in this step at all.

  • 2
    Pay the SEVIS I-901 fee yourself

    Only at fmjfee.com — this is a 5-minute form with a credit card, not something worth paying someone else $500+ to click through.

  • 3
    Fill out the DS-160 yourself

    At ceac.state.gov. It asks for information you already know about yourself — your history, your program, your finances. It's long, not hard.

  • 4
    Pay the visa fee and schedule your interview

    Through your embassy's official appointment system, linked from the DS-160 confirmation page.

  • 5
    Gather your documents

    See the real checklist — every item on it is something you already have or can get directly, no agent required.

  • 6
    Go to your interview

    Answer honestly about your program and your intent to return home after it. That's genuinely what's being assessed.
