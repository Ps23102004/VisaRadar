F-1 documents

SEVIS I-901 fee: $350

  • Form I-20Issued by your SEVP-certified school after admission
  • I-901 SEVIS fee receiptPay only at fmjfee.com — nowhere else
  • DS-160 confirmation pageOnline nonimmigrant visa application, at ceac.state.gov
  • Visa application fee receiptPaid through the official channel your embassy specifies
  • Valid passportValid at least 6 months beyond your intended stay
  • DS-160 photoMeeting the official photo specification
  • Financial evidenceBank statements or affidavit of support showing you can cover the program
  • Evidence of ties to home countryYou must affirmatively show non-immigrant intent (INA 214(b))

The interview

The State Department publishes no official "sample questions" list — anywhere you see one, it's a third party's guess, not DoS material, and VisaRadar won't pretend otherwise. What is official: under INA Section 214(b), every applicant must affirmatively demonstrate non-immigrant intent to the consular officer. That's what the interview is actually assessing.
